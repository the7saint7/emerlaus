Emerlaus Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Emerlaus may process when you use the Discord Activity and how that information is used.

1. Information We Process

To operate Emerlaus, the game may process limited information made available through Discord or your gameplay session, including:

  • your Discord user ID;
  • your display name and avatar;
  • Activity instance and match session identifiers;
  • gameplay state such as seat assignment, match actions, and connection status;
  • technical logs or error reports needed to diagnose problems.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate and support the game, including to:

  • authenticate players through Discord;
  • place players into matches and synchronize game state;
  • show player identity inside the match interface;
  • debug, secure, maintain, and improve the service.

3. Data Sharing

Emerlaus does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting, infrastructure, or platform providers only as needed to run the service, including Discord and the application's hosting provider.

4. Data Retention

Match data and session data may be stored temporarily for the duration of gameplay and for limited operational logging. Data may be deleted, reset, or overwritten at any time as part of normal service operation.

5. Security

Reasonable steps may be taken to protect information used by the service, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Emerlaus is not directed to children under the minimum age required to use Discord in the relevant jurisdiction.

7. Your Choices

If you do not want this information processed for gameplay, do not use the Activity. You may also stop using the Activity at any time and manage your Discord account settings through Discord.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Emerlaus after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy should be directed to the support contact provided with the Emerlaus application, website, or Discord listing.
