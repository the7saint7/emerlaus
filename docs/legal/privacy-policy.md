# Emerlaus Privacy Policy

This repo publishes the public Privacy Policy page used for the Discord Activity at:

- `/privacy`
- `/privacy.html`

The live HTML source is:

- `client/public/privacy.html`

When deployed on Railway, the Discord Developer Portal Privacy Policy URL can be:

- `https://<your-railway-domain>/privacy`

Before wider distribution, review the text and replace the generic support contact language with your real support email or support page if you have one.
