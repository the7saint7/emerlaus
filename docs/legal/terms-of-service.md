# Emerlaus Terms of Service

This repo publishes the public Terms of Service page used for the Discord Activity at:

- `/terms`
- `/terms.html`

The live HTML source is:

- `client/public/terms.html`

When deployed on Railway, the Discord Developer Portal Terms URL can be:

- `https://<your-railway-domain>/terms`

Before wider distribution, review the text and replace the generic support contact language with your real support email or support page if you have one.
